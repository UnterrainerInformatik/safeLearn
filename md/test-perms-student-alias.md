# Student Plural Alias Permission
The role `student` also satisfies a directive written in the plural, the same way `teacher` does. The alias belongs to the session, not to the directive, so naming `students` grants nothing that naming `student` would not grant.

The following paragraph is addressed with the plural spelling:
@@@ students
>Reached through the plural student alias.
@@@

The following paragraph is addressed with the singular spelling:
@@@ student
>Reached through the singular student role.
@@@

The following paragraph names the role and its alias together:
@@@ student, students
>Reached through the student role and its alias at once.
@@@
