# Plural Alias Permission
The role `teacher` also satisfies a directive written in the plural. The alias belongs to the session, not to the directive, so naming `teachers` grants nothing that naming `teacher` would not grant.

The following paragraph is addressed with the plural spelling:
@@@ teachers
>Reached through the plural alias.
@@@

The following paragraph names the role and its alias together:
@@@ teacher, teachers
>Reached through the role and the alias at once.
@@@
